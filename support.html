Support

Support is by email and is answered within two business days, usually much faster. The support address is published here when the storefront domain is set at launch; until then this page carries the answers to the questions every buyer asks.

How does the seasonal pause work?

Request a pause from the billing portal or by email. Billing stops, your calculator displays your business phone number instead of returning estimates, and your pricing rules and leads are kept exactly as they were. Resume whenever you are ready and everything comes back as it was. Pausing is free and you can do it once per season.

How do I cancel?

From the billing portal, or by email and we will do it for you. Your subscription runs to the end of the paid period. Your pricing rules and leads stay exportable for 90 days after cancellation, so leaving never costs you your own data.

What happens to my website if the service is down or my payment fails?

Your site is never left with a broken box. If the calculator cannot be reached, or a subscription is suspended after a failed payment and the grace period, the widget displays your business name and phone number instead. A card expiring in your busy season shows you a warning banner and a grace period first; it does not switch your pricing off without warning.

Who confirms the final price with my customer?

You do. The tool's estimates come from your pricing rules and are labelled as estimates to your visitor. The final price is always yours to confirm before work is booked, which is how the trades already run.
